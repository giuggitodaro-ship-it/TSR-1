## TS-04 Stabilisation

| Option | Added mass [kg] | Crane side-lift limit @2.56 m, 15° [kg] | Planned winch pull limit [kN] | Deploy time [min] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S0 locked wheels only | 0.0 | 380 | 0.98 | 1 |
| S1 body lowering (skid on ground) | 0.0 | 424 | 0.98 | 5 |
| S2 deployable lateral outriggers (±0.8 m) | 24.0 | 763 | 0.98 | 11 |
| S3 rear spades | 17.6 | 380 | 2.71 | 17 |
| S4 rear spades + 2 helical anchors | 25.6 | 380 | 3.95 | 67 |
| S5 body lowering + spades + 2 anchors | 25.6 | 424 | 3.95 | 71 |

**Findings.** For manipulation, locked wheels + differential lock already give tipping factor ≥ 1.5 at the crane's
150 kg rating on a 15° side slope (stability limit ≈ 380 kg): **outriggers are not
needed** (+24 kg rejected). For winching, the vehicle's lunar weight (≈ 2.02 kN) is far below the
desired line pull: braked wheels alone limit planned pulls to ≈ 1.0 kN; rear spades raise it to
≈ 2.7 kN; spades + 2 helical anchors with front hold-down straps to ≈ 3.9 kN.
A high fairlead (0.60 m) pitches the vehicle over at 4 kN (tipping factor < 1, case C7c); the **0.25 m fairlead** is a
design requirement.

**Decision:** locked wheels + differential lock for manipulation; rear spades + front helical anchors (S4/S5) for
recovery. Body lowering is retained for other reasons (TS-01).

**Findings.** Direct towing is useful only on near-level ground (Scenario A, D); on slopes the anchored winch is the
enabling element (B, B2, D2). Deep wheel embedding (C) is the hard case: feasible at the mid estimate with little
margin and infeasible at the upper-bound (Bekker compaction) estimate unless ramps are excavated first (C-dig) — which
justifies the regolith scoop tool. **Decision: R4 (4 kN winch, 50 m Vectran line, 2 rear spades, 2 helical anchors + 2
spare in MOD-REC).** R5 (4 anchors mounted) gains ≈ 0.02 in p_env and is not worth permanently carried hardware.
Physical caveat: anchor and spade capacities are first-principles estimates (no lunar data, technology gap TG-03).
