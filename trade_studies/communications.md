A DRM's approval-critical data (~50 MB of imagery/LiDAR per hold point set) moves over S-band relay in ≈ 15–20 min
worst case; bulk data waits for the surface network (DTN store-and-forward). **Decision: surface network + LunaNet
S-band relay + UHF mesh + DTN** (availability ≈ 0.98); Ka-band
rejected (pointing mechanism in dust, power) because approval latency, not bandwidth, limits productivity; a
deployable repeater module (MOD-RPT) covers PSR excursions.
