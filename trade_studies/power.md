**Decision: Li-ion PPR battery 15 kWh usable at EOL (23.6 kWh nameplate,
28s67p, 148 kg) + 1.5 m² fixed vertical PV + grid charging at ISPSIS nodes.**
Sized for DRM-2 at the 10 km service edge without PV credit. Vertical PV (166 W average in sun) exceeds the
survival power (82 W): a TSR-1 stranded in sunlight survives indefinitely (closure ENERGY-3).
Full-battery survival in darkness: 183 h (≥ 120 h requirement, S030). Radioisotope power (E3)
is technically attractive (indefinite PSR operation, waste heat) but rejected for availability/approval reasons for an
independent programme; it is the recommended variant if a PSR-intensive mission is prioritised.

**Emergency power capability (directive §6C).** At the 10 km edge, after the outbound traverse and keeping return energy
and the reserve: 10.6 h of 300 W keep-alive or 1.4 h at the full
3 kW rating. Longer support is provided by leaving a MOD-KA keep-alive module (TS-07). Cable: 25 m tether, 2 ×
6.9 mm² Cu for 3 % drop at 3 kW (90 W loss). Charging 3 kW → 14 kWh in ≈ 6 h.

**Key sensitivity:** DRM energy is dominated by hotel loads during slow autonomous traverse, so **effective speed**
is the largest driver (tornado: 0.7 → 2.5 km/h changes DRM-2 energy by ≈ 2×) — faster verified autonomy saves more
battery than better motors.
