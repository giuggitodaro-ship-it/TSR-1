| Ti-6Al-4V tube | 27.8 | 3.24 | 25.7 | 3.23 |

**Findings.** Both the chassis (minimum-gauge driven) and the arms (actuator-mass dominated) are insensitive to
structural material choice: CFRP saves ≈ 15 kg on the chassis and ≈ 1–2 kg per arm. Selection therefore rests on
durability across ~3650 thermal cycles between ≈ 40 K and 390 K, conductive heat spreading in the WEB, impact and
abrasion tolerance, CTE compatibility at joints, and heritage. **Selected:** Al 7075-T7351/Al 5056 honeycomb
chassis with Ti-6Al-4V hard points; Ti-6Al-4V dexterous-arm links; CFRP only for the long, buckling-critical crane boom
and the mast. The full matrix (40 application/candidate rows incl. gears, bearings, lubricants, seals, cables,
connectors, fasteners, insulation, radiators, tool heads) is `engineering/materials_matrix.csv`.
