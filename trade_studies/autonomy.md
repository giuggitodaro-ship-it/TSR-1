Model (`trades/other_trades.autonomy_task_time`): teleoperation = 300 discrete motions × (8 s round-trip incl.
processing + 10 s operator decision + 15 s motion) / DTE availability 0.51; supervised autonomy = 2 h execution +
8 hold-point approvals × 10 min.

**Decision: AU3 supervised autonomy** — verified skills executed onboard with local force/torque loops, a
deterministic safety layer, and human approval at irreversible steps. Earth teleoperation is retained only as a
low-rate "point-and-go" contingency; local crew teleoperation is a backup when crew are present. Full autonomy (AU4)
is rejected because novel-fault handling cannot be verified to the level required near crew and high-value assets.

**AI/ML components (directive §21):**

| Component | Why deterministic software is insufficient | Training/data | Verification boundary | Confidence handling | Fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Terrain classification (soft soil, rocks) | appearance-based hazards under extreme lighting are hard to hand-code | analogue-site + lunar imagery (VIPER/CADRE/Chang'e), simulated long-shadow renders | output is a cost map consumed by a geometric planner with hard LiDAR obstacle limits | per-cell uncertainty → slow down / stop | geometric-only planning at reduced speed |
| Visual anomaly detection (cracks, dust, connector damage) | open-ended anomaly appearance | asset-owner reference imagery + synthetic defects | advisory: flags for human review; never triggers an irreversible action | score + calibrated threshold | human inspection of imagery |
| Pose estimation for docking/connector mating | robust under specular, low-sun conditions | fiducial + CAD-based synthetic training | must agree with fiducial/LiDAR pose within tolerance before contact | disagreement → abort to stand-off | fiducial-only pose |
| Fault diagnosis ranking | combinatorial fault trees of heterogeneous assets | asset FMEA/telemetry models | ranks hypotheses; tests are deterministic electrical measurements | top-k with probabilities | operator-selected diagnosis |
| Task-plan proposal (LLM/planner hybrid) | natural-language asset manuals, novel combinations | asset documentation | output must compile to the verified-skill grammar and pass a deterministic plan checker | rejected plans returned to humans | DRM templates |
