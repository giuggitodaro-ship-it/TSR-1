### Dexterous service arm (×2, identical)
```
COMPONENT:            Dexterous service arm (×2, identical)
SUBSYSTEM:            manipulation
FUNCTION:             ORU ≤ 20 kg swaps, connector/fastener work, probing, cleaning, tow rigging, anchor installation, guiding crane loads; bimanual tasks

SELECTED TECHNOLOGY:  7-DOF serial arm (3 pitch joints + 4 roll/yaw), 1.6 m reach, wrist 6-axis F/T sensor, tool changer, macro camera
SELECTED MATERIAL(S): Ti-6Al-4V link tubes and joint housings; strain-wave gears; BMG gears in wrist joints; MoS2

WHY THIS TECHNOLOGY:  TS-03: two identical dexterous arms + crane give 0.96 weighted task coverage at 71 kg vs 158 kg for heavy+dex; second arm removes the arm SPF (λ 0.08/yr)
WHY THIS MATERIAL:    arm mass actuator-dominated; Ti tougher for contact work and avoids bonded CFRP joints over 40–390 K

ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED: one general 100 kg arm; heavy+dexterous asymmetric pair; single dex arm + crane
WHY REJECTED:         general arm 102 kg with 0.83 coverage; asymmetric pair +113 kg for +4 % coverage; single arm leaves SPF

DIMENSIONS:           links 0.75/0.70/0.15 m; shoulder Ø≈0.12 m
MASS:                 CBE 53.9 kg; predicted 67.4 kg incl. MGA (2 configuration item(s); engineering/mass_budget.csv)
OPERATING POWER:      inspection 16 W, manipulation 53 W, heavy_manip 79 W
PEAK POWER:           158 W (sum of item peaks)

OPERATING TEMPERATURE: operate −55/+70 °C (BMG wrist to −173 °C, S040)
MECHANICAL LOADS:     20 kg rated payload at full reach (lunar g), tip deflection ≤ 3 mm; ≤ 150 N contact force
RADIATION CONSIDERATIONS: joint electronics in WEB except encoders (rad-tolerant)
DUST CONSIDERATIONS:  joint bellows boots + labyrinths; EDS on camera window

EXPECTED LIFE:        10 yr; λ 0.08/yr per arm (ESTIMATE)
REDUNDANCY:           two arms
FAILURE MODES:        joint/brake/encoder failure (F-05)
MAINTENANCE METHOD:   arm-level ORU at base (II-03); joint ORUs

TRL:                  5 (space arms TRL 9 in µg/Mars; lunar dust + cold joints TRL 4–5)
HERITAGE:             M2020 arm, Dextre, xLink, COLDArm

CALCULATED VALUES:    CALCULATED manipulation.arm/crane sizing (torque density, CFRP tubes, MF)
ASSUMPTIONS:          motorisation factor 2 (L024), torque density 25 N·m/kg
UNCERTAINTIES:        actuator mass ±40 %; contact-task success rates (servicing.py)
```
