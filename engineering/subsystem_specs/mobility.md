### Wheel (×6)
```
COMPONENT:            Wheel (×6)
SUBSYSTEM:            mobility
FUNCTION:             Support vehicle load, generate traction, roll over regolith and rocks

SELECTED TECHNOLOGY:  Rigid wheel, Ø0.90 m × 0.40 m, 18 Ti grousers h = 20 mm, internal flexure spokes
SELECTED MATERIAL(S): Ti-6Al-4V rim (1.2 mm skin) and spokes; plasma-nitrided grouser tips

WHY THIS TECHNOLOGY:  Contact pressure 6.6 kPa ≤ 7 kPa rule (S023) with DP/W(40 %) ≥ 0.36 at lowest wheel mass in the TS-02 sweep; rigid-wheel terramechanics applies (model confidence)
WHY THIS MATERIAL:    Toughness and fatigue strength at 40–390 K, abrasion resistance after nitriding; Al rims suffered rock damage on MSL

ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED: Ø0.8 × 0.35 m (pressure 7.1 kPa at final mass), LRV wire mesh, NiTi spring tyre, Al 7075 rigid wheel
WHY REJECTED:         smaller wheel violated 7 kPa at final mass; mesh/NiTi: dust ingress, unproven 10-yr fatigue, low model confidence (NiTi kept as upgrade); Al: damage history

DIMENSIONS:           Ø0.90 m, width 0.40 m, grouser height 0.020 m
MASS:                 CBE 62.4 kg; predicted 77.9 kg incl. MGA (6 configuration item(s); engineering/mass_budget.csv)
OPERATING POWER:      none (passive)
PEAK POWER:           0 W (sum of item peaks)

OPERATING TEMPERATURE: −230 °C to +120 °C (non-operating extremes); no limit for operation
MECHANICAL LOADS:     static wheel load ≈ 330 N (lunar); design 3 g lunar dynamic + launch 6 g/3 g on locks
RADIATION CONSIDERATIONS: none (metallic)
DUST CONSIDERATIONS:  no enclosed volume; grousers shed regolith; fender + skirt limit ejecta

EXPECTED LIFE:        10 yr / ≥ 1500 km traverse (no flight data at this distance)
REDUNDANCY:           6 wheels; mobility with 4/6 driven (13.6°)
FAILURE MODES:        rim crack (fatigue), grouser wear, spoke buckling on rock impact
MAINTENANCE METHOD:   wheel+drive ORU at hub flange (II-04); body lowering unloads wheel

TRL:                  6 (rigid lunar/Mars wheels flown; this size/material in lunar dust not qualified)
HERITAGE:             VIPER, Yutu, Pragyan, MSL/M2020 rigid wheels

SOURCE DATA:          see research/source_register.csv (IDs cited above)
CALCULATED VALUES:    CALCULATED configuration._wheel_mass (Ti-6Al-4V rim+spokes+grousers)
ASSUMPTIONS:          A-03 soil; wheel_rim_t ESTIMATE
UNCERTAINTIES:        rim thickness for 10-yr fatigue (±50 %); grouser effectiveness (first-order model)
```

### Wheel drive actuator (×6)
```
COMPONENT:            Wheel drive actuator (×6)
SUBSYSTEM:            mobility
FUNCTION:             Drive wheel; hold vehicle with power-off brake

SELECTED TECHNOLOGY:  Frameless BLDC motor + strain-wave gear (~100:1) + power-off brake + absolute encoder, in-hub
SELECTED MATERIAL(S): Ti-6Al-4V housing; 15-5PH/maraging flexspline, 440C circular spline; hybrid Si3N4 bearings; MoS2 + Braycote 601EF

WHY THIS TECHNOLOGY:  LRV used 80:1 harmonic drive per wheel (S027); compact, backlash-free, high ratio
WHY THIS MATERIAL:    Ti housing CTE close to steel gear/bearing set (preload stability); dry + PFPE lubrication for cold start

ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED: planetary gearbox; direct-drive torque motor; hydraulic
WHY REJECTED:         planetary: backlash/mass; direct drive: mass ×3 for the torque; hydraulics: vacuum/cold incompatibility

DIMENSIONS:           ≈ Ø0.22 × 0.18 m
MASS:                 CBE 53.9 kg; predicted 67.4 kg incl. MGA (6 configuration item(s); engineering/mass_budget.csv)
OPERATING POWER:      driving 218 W
PEAK POWER:           1461 W (sum of item peaks)

OPERATING TEMPERATURE: operate −55 °C to +70 °C (warm start from −120 °C via winding self-heating)
MECHANICAL LOADS:     rated torque = soil-limited max (20° climb, heaviest wheel, i = 0.6) × 1.25
RADIATION CONSIDERATIONS: encoder/driver electronics in WEB; motor passive
DUST CONSIDERATIONS:  labyrinth + PTFE bellows at output; no exposed dynamic elastomers

EXPECTED LIFE:        10 yr, > 2×10^7 output revolutions at derated torque
REDUNDANCY:           6 units; free-wheel clutch on brake failure
FAILURE MODES:        gear wear, brake stuck, encoder fault (F-01)
MAINTENANCE METHOD:   ORU with wheel at hub

TRL:                  5
HERITAGE:             LRV traction drives, MSL/M2020 wheel actuators

SOURCE DATA:          see research/source_register.csv (IDs cited above)
CALCULATED VALUES:    CALCULATED: soil-limited torque × margin / torque density (act_torque_density)
ASSUMPTIONS:          act_torque_density 25 N·m/kg (ESTIMATE)
UNCERTAINTIES:        torque density 15–40 N·m/kg → ±40 % mass
```

### Steering actuator (×6)
```
COMPONENT:            Steering actuator (×6)
SUBSYSTEM:            mobility
FUNCTION:             Steer each wheel ±90° (crab, point turn, Ackermann)

SELECTED TECHNOLOGY:  BLDC + strain-wave gear + brake; spring-centring to 0°
SELECTED MATERIAL(S): Ti-6Al-4V/Al housing, steel gears, MoS2

WHY THIS TECHNOLOGY:  all-wheel explicit steering avoids skid-steer scrubbing torque in loose regolith
WHY THIS MATERIAL:    as drive actuator

ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED: skid steering; 4-wheel steering only
WHY REJECTED:         skid steering: high scrub energy and dust generation; 4-wheel: larger turning radius on a 3.6 m vehicle

DIMENSIONS:           ≈ Ø0.14 × 0.14 m
MASS:                 CBE 25.5 kg; predicted 31.8 kg incl. MGA (6 configuration item(s); engineering/mass_budget.csv)
OPERATING POWER:      driving 18 W
PEAK POWER:           159 W (sum of item peaks)

OPERATING TEMPERATURE: as drive actuator
MECHANICAL LOADS:     scrub torque μN·√(l²+b²)/3 × 1.5 × MF 2
RADIATION CONSIDERATIONS: as drive
DUST CONSIDERATIONS:  as drive

EXPECTED LIFE:        10 yr
REDUNDANCY:           5 of 6 sufficient; skid fallback
FAILURE MODES:        stuck at angle (F-02)
MAINTENANCE METHOD:   ORU at bogie

TRL:                  5
HERITAGE:             MSL/M2020 steering actuators

SOURCE DATA:          see research/source_register.csv (IDs cited above)
CALCULATED VALUES:    CALCULATED: scrub torque μN·√(l²+b²)/3 ×1.5 × MF / torque density
ASSUMPTIONS:          scrub-torque model ESTIMATE
UNCERTAINTIES:        steering torque in compacted ruts ±100 %
```

### Rocker-bogie suspension with body lowering and differential lock
```
COMPONENT:            Rocker-bogie suspension with body lowering and differential lock
SUBSYSTEM:            mobility
FUNCTION:             Equalise wheel loads on rough terrain; lower chassis onto belly skid; lock body attitude for manipulation

SELECTED TECHNOLOGY:  Passive 6-wheel rocker-bogie (differential bar), two lead-screw actuators at rocker pivots lowering the body 0.35 m, differential brake
SELECTED MATERIAL(S): Al 7075-T7351 links, Ti-6Al-4V pivots, BMG lead-screw nuts

WHY THIS TECHNOLOGY:  TS-01: same slope capability as active designs (traction-limited, 19.8°) at 80–100 kg less mass; lowering enables wheel-drive ORU swap and lower CoM
WHY THIS MATERIAL:    stiff, light, machinable Al links; Ti pivots for bearing CTE compatibility

ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED: passive rocker-bogie; fully active 6-leg; 4-wheel active
WHY REJECTED:         passive: no wheel unloading for self-repair; active: +80–100 kg, +3–6 actuators, no slope gain

DIMENSIONS:           wheelbase 2.6 m, track 2.0 m, clearance 0.45 m (0.10 m lowered)
MASS:                 CBE 69.6 kg; predicted 83.9 kg incl. MGA (4 configuration item(s); engineering/mass_budget.csv)
OPERATING POWER:      none (passive)
PEAK POWER:           140 W (sum of item peaks)

OPERATING TEMPERATURE: structure: any; actuators as drive actuator
MECHANICAL LOADS:     half vehicle weight per lowering actuator × 1.5 dynamic × MF 2
RADIATION CONSIDERATIONS: n/a
DUST CONSIDERATIONS:  sealed pivots; actuators booted

EXPECTED LIFE:        10 yr
REDUNDANCY:           lowering actuator fails safe at drive height
FAILURE MODES:        pivot seizure (F-03), lowering actuator stuck (F-04)
MAINTENANCE METHOD:   links not ORUs; actuators ORU

TRL:                  6 (rocker-bogie TRL 9 on Mars; lowering feature TRL 5)
HERITAGE:             MER, MSL, M2020

SOURCE DATA:          see research/source_register.csv (IDs cited above)
CALCULATED VALUES:    CALCULATED: 0.5 W ×1.5 × MF / 1.5 kN/kg (ESTIMATE); ESTIMATE: 4.5 % of operational mass (links Al 7075 / Ti pivots) + 6 pivot bearings; ESTIMATE: brake on rocker differential (body attitude lock for manipulation)
ASSUMPTIONS:          suspension 4.5 % of operational mass (ESTIMATE)
UNCERTAINTIES:        link mass ±30 %
```
