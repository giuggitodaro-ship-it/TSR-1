### Recovery winch and line
```
COMPONENT:            Recovery winch and line
SUBSYSTEM:            recovery
FUNCTION:             Pull immobilised vehicles/elements while TSR-1 is anchored

SELECTED TECHNOLOGY:  4 kN electric winch, drum Ø0.12 m, level-wind, load cell, auto tension limiting; 50 m Vectran line; low fairlead (0.25 m)
SELECTED MATERIAL(S): Ti drum, steel gear, Vectran with aramid/PTFE jacket

WHY THIS TECHNOLOGY:  TS-05: anchored winching raises recoverable fraction from 0.27 (direct tow) to 0.92
WHY THIS MATERIAL:    line mass 0.03 kg/m vs 0.15 kg/m steel rope; low creep

ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED: direct towing only; steel rope
WHY REJECTED:         traction-limited (215 N drawbar pull on 15°); 5× line mass

DIMENSIONS:           ≈ 0.45 × 0.3 × 0.3 m
MASS:                 CBE 25.7 kg; predicted 32.0 kg incl. MGA (2 configuration item(s); engineering/mass_budget.csv)
OPERATING POWER:      dormant 0 W, recovery_winch 185 W
PEAK POWER:           400 W (sum of item peaks)

OPERATING TEMPERATURE: operate −55/+70 °C
MECHANICAL LOADS:     4 kN line pull × FoS 1.4 at hard point; snap-back keep-out
RADIATION CONSIDERATIONS: n/a
DUST CONSIDERATIONS:  fairlead wiper; drum cover

EXPECTED LIFE:        10 yr; 500 pulls
REDUNDANCY:           none (DM-5)
FAILURE MODES:        F-07, F-08
MAINTENANCE METHOD:   ORU; spare line in MOD-REC

TRL:                  5 (terrestrial winches TRL 9; lunar vacuum/dust TRL 4–5)

```

### Ground-reaction system: rear spades, helical anchors, hard points
```
COMPONENT:            Ground-reaction system: rear spades, helical anchors, hard points
SUBSYSTEM:            recovery
FUNCTION:             Resist line pull and overturning moment during winching

SELECTED TECHNOLOGY:  2 rear spades 0.6 × 0.3 m (pressed by body lowering, self-embedding under pull); 2 Ø0.15 m helical anchors at 0.6 m depth installed by dex arm; front hold-down straps
SELECTED MATERIAL(S): Ti-6Al-4V with TiN edges

WHY THIS TECHNOLOGY:  M-5: 4 kN pull is 2.0× TSR lunar weight (2024 N); spades+anchors give the restraint and tipping factor ≥ 1.5 (stability case C7a/b)
WHY THIS MATERIAL:    strength/mass, non-magnetic

ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED: outriggers; braked wheels only; 4 anchors
WHY REJECTED:         outriggers do not resist sliding; braked wheels ≈1.6 kN only; 4 anchors +0.02 p_env

DIMENSIONS:           spade plate 0.6 × 0.3 × 0.006 m; anchor shaft 0.7 m Ø25 mm
MASS:                 CBE 24.6 kg; predicted 30.6 kg incl. MGA (5 configuration item(s); engineering/mass_budget.csv)
OPERATING POWER:      none (passive)
PEAK POWER:           120 W (sum of item peaks)

OPERATING TEMPERATURE: any
MECHANICAL LOADS:     spade 1.3 kN each (P50 soil); anchor 0.92 kN axial; install torque 28 N·m
RADIATION CONSIDERATIONS: n/a
DUST CONSIDERATIONS:  n/a (ground-engaging)

EXPECTED LIFE:        10 yr; anchors reusable
REDUNDANCY:           spare anchors in MOD-REC
FAILURE MODES:        F-09, F-10
MAINTENANCE METHOD:   spades ORU

TRL:                  3–4 (no lunar regolith anchor data: literature gap)
HERITAGE:             terrestrial helical anchors (Hoyt & Clemence L026), recovery-vehicle spades

SOURCE DATA:          see research/source_register.csv (IDs cited above)
CALCULATED VALUES:    CALCULATED: Ti plate 0.6×0.3×6 mm + deploy actuator/hinge 4 kg (ESTIMATE); ESTIMATE: Ti shaft 0.7 m Ø25 mm + Ø150 mm helix; ESTIMATE: Ti-6Al-4V lugs, pins
ASSUMPTIONS:          A-26, helix_Nq, spade_shape_factor ESTIMATES
UNCERTAINTIES:        capacity ±50 % (weak soil p_env 0.83); refusal on clasts
```
